# Open decisions

## Hosting

- exact OVH plan and available access: FTP/SFTP, SSH, database, scheduled tasks, and server configuration;
- current WordPress content and whether it must be retained;
- technical ability to serve an application under `/familiy-tree`;
- backup mechanism already provided by OVH.

## Product

- person or couple initially centred when the tree opens;
- initial orientation: ancestors, descendants, or mixed view;
- estimated number of people and media files;
- display rules for living people;
- people authorised to administer data or suggest corrections;
- required languages beyond the French interface.

## Data

- quality and format of available scans;
- existence of a previous GEDCOM file or digital tree;
- conventions used in the notes for uncertain dates and sources;
- selected private location for originals and backups.

## Visual identity

- desired logo, colours, typography, and atmosphere;
- photographs or documents that may be used with consent in the final interface.
