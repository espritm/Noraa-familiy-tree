# Product vision

## Summary

Noraä Family Tree enables authorised relatives to explore their family history through a visual family tree, individual profiles, photographs, and sources collected from French archives.

## Audience

- relatives, including people who are not comfortable with complex digital tools;
- administrators responsible for entering and correcting information;
- occasional contributors suggesting a photograph, correction, or source.

## Intended experience

The tree must remain understandable across many generations and branches. Users can zoom and pan horizontally and vertically, recenter the view, find a person, and visually follow ancestors, descendants, partners, and siblings.

A compact card displays the primary photograph, given names, birth name, current or customary name, and lifespan. Selecting it opens a detailed profile without losing the current position in the tree.

## Design principles

1. The family tree remains the central experience rather than becoming a data table.
2. Unknown, approximate, or conflicting dates are represented honestly.
3. Every historical claim can be linked to a source.
4. Living people receive stronger privacy protection.
5. The interface works with keyboard, mouse, and touch input.
6. Performance remains acceptable on mobile devices and with a large tree.

## Initial success criteria

- reach a known person within three actions after opening the tree;
- keep navigation fluid with at least 500 people;
- display profiles and primary photographs promptly over a mobile connection;
- restore private data successfully from a documented backup;
- never publish real family data in the public source repository.
